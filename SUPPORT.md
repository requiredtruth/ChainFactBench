## Request a direction

After confirmation, open the funded-direction issue with the asset, network, public transaction hash, and specific benchmark, evaluator, evidence, or compatibility direction requested. The first issue claiming an unclaimed confirmed inbound transaction hash receives operational request attribution. This is not cryptographic proof of wallet ownership, and an attributed hash cannot be reused.

A donation can direct a request, but it does not purchase ownership, investment returns, a delivery deadline, support priority, or acceptance of unsafe, illegal, private, proprietary, custodial, trading, or out-of-scope work.
